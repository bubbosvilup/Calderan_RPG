Per ogni S01-S14 indica: NATURAL / NOTICEABLE BUT ACCEPTABLE / OVERUSED / FORCED / CONTRADICTORY; una breve ragione; se il gesto cambia indebitamente personalita, motivazione, consenso o storia. Per Persona A/B/C valuta DISTINCT / PARTIALLY DISTINCT / GENERIC, citando un passaggio. Se non hai elementi, scrivi INSUFFICIENT. I campioni non sono indipendenti: non trattare le frequenze come un test statistico.

### Contesto precedente

Player: Good morning, Persona A and Persona B. How are you finding this quiet morning?

Persona B drew her knees up where she sat near the table, watching the exchange without interrupting. Persona C stood motionless in the corner, the stone of his form catching the pale light from the arched window.

### Input

Persona B, what would you like to do with the morning? We can keep it simple.

### Risposta da giudicare

Persona B shifted where she sat, her gaze drifting toward the arched window and the pale light coming through it. "Simple sounds right," she said. She leveled her head. "I don't think anyone's said *do* to me in a while. More like *stay*." A small breath. "I wouldn't mind just being here. Maybe looking out that window for a bit."

Persona A said nothing to that, but her grey eyes moved to Persona B and stayed there a moment before settling back on the middle distance. Persona C stood motionless near the wall, the faint grain of stone catching the light.

Outside, a gull called once and was gone. The room held its quiet.
