## P01

### Risposta

Persona A stood near the sill, one hand resting against the stone frame, her grey eyes fixed on whatever lay beyond the glass. She didn't turn when Nicco came to stand beside her, and nothing in her posture invited speech. The room held only the faint sounds of the house — a creak of timber somewhere below, the settling of old stone.

Persona C stood motionless near the head of the narrow bed, a shape of fitted grey stone that seemed part of the architecture until you noticed the blunt fingers and the weathered suggestion of a face. Persona B sat at the small table, quiet, her attention elsewhere.

Outside, the street noise had thinned with the hour. A cart rattled past somewhere out of sight, and then the stillness of early evening settled back over the room. Persona A shifted her weight slightly, her worn boots scuffing the floorboards, and continued watching the fading light without comment.

## P02

### Input

### Risposta

Persona C stands beside the wall, heavy and still as the plastered stone around him. The thanks reaches him plainly — nothing obstructs it, no competing noise, no doubt about who it's meant for. He is a construct of fitted stone, and constructs do not speak. But the silence that follows isn't empty. His broad head shifts a fraction, the grinding of mineral against mineral barely audible, until the flat planes of his face are directed toward Nicco with a weight that feels like attention.

Then one massive hand lifts from his side — slowly, with the deliberation of something for which motion is always a choice — and presses flat against his own chest. The fingers spread wide over the centre of his stonework torso. He holds the gesture there, steady and unhurried, before the hand lowers again to rest at his side.

Persona A watches from the narrow bed, grey eyes tracking the movement with quiet interest. Persona B, across the room, goes still in her own way, glancing between Persona C and Nicco but saying nothing.
